The Problem

What happens when project controls are weak?

Schedule uncertainty

Milestones move every cycle; the completion date is a guess.

Unreliable progress

Every party reports different numbers for the same scope.

Cost visibility gaps

Commitments and actuals diverge until it is too late to act.

Forecast instability

Estimates at completion change with every reporting round.

Fragmented contractor reporting

Different formats and metrics prevent a consolidated view.
