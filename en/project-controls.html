The Client Problem

Large infrastructure programmes often produce activity and reporting, but leadership lacks a reliable picture of where the programme really stands. Schedules drift, cost visibility weakens, risks are reported too late, and recovery becomes reactive.

What I Do

Establish practical controls that create one reliable view of programme performance.

I design and operate planning, cost, risk and reporting systems that give programme leadership early warning, accurate forecasts and the ability to make decisions before problems become unrecoverable.

What I Deliver

Key deliverables for programme control

Planning & Scheduling

Integrated master schedules with baseline management, critical path analysis and resource-loaded plans in Primavera P6 or MS Project.

Progress Measurement

Objective progress measurement methods that align physical progress with schedule and cost performance.

Cost Control

Budget baseline, commitment tracking, actual cost capture and variance analysis to protect programme margin.

EVM

Earned Value Management with SPI, CPI, EAC and VAC to provide an integrated schedule and cost view.

Forecasting

Realistic forecasts based on current performance trends, risk exposure and corrective actions in progress.

Risk & Change Control

Risk registers, change-control logs, impact assessments and structured escalation paths.

KPI Reporting

Focused KPIs linked to programme objectives, not vanity metrics, with clear ownership and review rhythms.

Executive Dashboards

Decision-ready dashboards in Power BI, Excel or project-specific tools that summarise what matters to leadership.
