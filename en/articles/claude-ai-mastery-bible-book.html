Claude AI Mastery Bible: My New Book Is Now on Amazon

By Ashraf Ibrahim El Desoky· Nov 26, 2026· 4 min read

I'm pleased to share that my new book, Claude AI Mastery Bible: The Complete 5-in-1 Guide to Claude AI, Cowork, Skills, MCP, Sub-Agents, Claude Code, AI Automation, and Building Profitable AI-Powered Businesses, is now available on Amazon in paperback and Kindle editions — published under my pen name, Donald Johnson.

Why Five Books in One

Two years ago, "using Claude" meant typing into a chat box. Today it is an entire ecosystem: a conversational assistant, an agent that works directly on your files, reusable expertise packages, a universal integration protocol, self-delegating sub-agents, a software-building agent, and a platform you can build real products on. No single short guide can cover that shift honestly — so this book is structured as five connected books that follow the natural learning path: understand, use, automate, build, monetize.

What Is Inside

Book One takes you from complete beginner to a confident, productive Claude user — how large language models actually work, the RTCF prompting framework, professional techniques like chain-of-thought and prompt chaining, vision and document analysis, live-web research, and writing workflows. Book Two covers Cowork — Claude's agentic mode that reads, creates and transforms real files on your machine, turning repetitive document work into delegated automation. Book Three is the customization deep-dive: building reusable Skills, connecting tools through the Model Context Protocol, and orchestrating sub-agents — written so non-developers can follow every step. Book Four teaches Claude Code through six hands-on projects, ending with a working mini-product — a receipt-to-expense tracker you actually build. Book Five closes the loop: turning these skills into income, from freelancing and productized services to a sustainable AI-powered business.

How the Book Teaches

Every chapter follows the same practical rhythm: objectives, explanation, a guided tutorial, a real-world scenario, common mistakes, troubleshooting, exercises and a summary. Each of the five books ends with a Final Project — the proof that the skill is actually yours, not just understood. And because this field moves fast, the book is honest about its limits: where the product and the book differ, the product is right, and it tells you where to check the current docs.

Free Companion Resources

The book comes with a full companion kit at no extra cost: editable worksheets and templates for every Final Project, the Skills and workflow files used in Books Two and Three, and a complete prompt library you can copy, adapt and reuse immediately.

Who It Is For

You do not need to be technical. This book was written for everyone who has been told AI is "too technical" for them — managers, engineers, consultants, freelancers and business owners who want to move from curiosity to real capability. Whether your goal is personal productivity, automating the grind of your job, building software, or starting a service business, the path inside is the same — and it starts at page one.
