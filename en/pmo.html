The Governance Problem

Large programmes generate large volumes of data, yet executives still struggle to see whether the programme is genuinely on track. Governance meetings become reporting rituals. Decisions are delayed because accountability, escalation paths and KPIs are unclear.

What I Provide

Practical PMO and governance that makes decisions clearer and accountability stronger.

I set up PMO frameworks, KPIs, review cycles, reporting and escalation paths that fit the programme rather than adding bureaucracy. The result is a governance system that supports decisions, surfaces problems early and keeps the programme aligned to its objectives.
