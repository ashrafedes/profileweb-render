02 / Project & Programme Management

Bring complex telecom and infrastructure projects and programmes under control.

What I Deliver

Large infrastructure programmes often have activity and reporting, but management lacks a reliable picture of actual performance. I establish the controls, governance and recovery discipline needed to restore visibility and decision-making.

Outcome

One reliable view of programme performance, clearer accountability, earlier warning of deviations, and a structured path back to control.

How I Help

I build practical control and governance systems that fit the programme rather than adding bureaucracy. When recovery is needed, I use a diagnosis-led approach with realistic targets.
