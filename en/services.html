Services

Three core service areas for complex telecom and infrastructure programmes, delivered at programme-management level across the MENA region.

📊

Project Controls

Schedule, cost, progress and risk disciplines that give leadership an honest, real-time picture of programme status.

  • Integrated master schedule and baseline management
  • Progress measurement, earned value management (EVM), S‑curves
  • Cost control, budgeting, forecasting and variance analysis
  • Risk and issue tracking with action-driven registers
  • Subcontractor and vendor performance dashboards
🏛

PMO & Governance

Frameworks, rhythms and reporting structures that align portfolios, standardise decisions and protect margins.

  • PMO setup, charters, stage-gates and governance models
  • KPI frameworks and executive reporting cadence
  • Project lifecycle methodology and templates
  • Resource planning, interface management and benefits tracking
  • Steering-committee and board-level reporting packs
🔄

Programme Recovery

Rapid diagnosis and stabilisation of programmes that are off track, before slippage becomes unrecoverable.

  • Independent health check and root-cause diagnosis
  • Recovery planning: schedule, cost, scope and stakeholder alignment
  • Work-stream prioritisation and fast-track re-baselining
  • Controls catch-up: reporting, risk and change discipline
  • Transition to steady-state governance
