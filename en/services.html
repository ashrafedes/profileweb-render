Services for Bringing Complex Projects & Programmes Under Control

I help organisations improve project performance, strengthen programme governance, and recover projects that are delayed or underperforming.

01

Project Controls

Bring schedule, cost, progress, risk, and performance under control.

Problem

Projects may have activity and reporting, but management may still lack a reliable view of actual performance.

Approach

Establish practical project-control processes for planning, progress measurement, forecasting, performance monitoring, and management reporting.

Key Deliverables

  • Planning & Scheduling
  • Progress Measurement
  • Cost Control
  • Earned Value Management (EVM)
  • Risk & Change Control
  • Forecasting
  • KPI Reporting
  • Executive Dashboards

Outcome

Better visibility of project performance, earlier identification of deviations, and stronger management decision support.

02

PMO & Programme Governance

Build a practical governance system that provides visibility, accountability, and control across complex programmes.

Problem

Multiple projects can operate with inconsistent reporting, unclear accountability, and fragmented management information.

Approach

Establish a practical governance and reporting framework appropriate to the programme.

Key Deliverables

  • PMO Framework
  • Governance Structure
  • Roles & Responsibilities
  • KPI Framework
  • Reporting Standards
  • Executive Dashboards
  • Review Cycles
  • Escalation Mechanisms
  • Programme Controls

Outcome

More consistent management information, clearer accountability, and stronger decision support.

03

Programme Recovery

Recover delayed or underperforming programmes through structured diagnosis, corrective action, and performance control.

Problem

A programme is delayed, underperforming, or losing control.

Approach

Assess the current position, identify the causes of deviation, and establish a practical recovery plan.

Key Deliverables

  • Current-State Assessment
  • Performance Diagnosis
  • Root-Cause Analysis
  • Schedule Assessment
  • Recovery Planning
  • Corrective Action Planning
  • Re-baselining where appropriate
  • Recovery Monitoring
  • Management Reporting

Outcome

A structured and measurable path toward restoring programme control.
