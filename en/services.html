Operational Network

The Challenge

Fibre and infrastructure rollouts involve thousands of routes, permits, civil works interfaces, contractor handovers, and high-volume unit-based progress. Without strong technical oversight, small field issues compound into schedule and cost problems that are discovered too late.

What I Deliver

  • Network planning and rollout strategy
  • FTTH / FTTx design review and optimisation
  • OSP route engineering and survey support
  • Civil works and duct infrastructure coordination
  • ISP / access network integration
  • As-built documentation and route validation
  • Testing and commissioning support
  • Contractor technical interface management

Expected Value

Clearer field execution, fewer design and interface surprises, and a network that is documented and ready for operations.

How I Help

I work alongside delivery teams to translate programme objectives into practical engineering decisions — from route planning and design review to field coordination and handover documentation.

02 / Project & Programme Management

Project Controls · PMO · Programme Recovery

Bring complex programmes under control — from visibility and governance to recovery when delivery has gone off track.

The Challenge

Large infrastructure programmes often have activity and reporting, but management lacks a reliable picture of actual performance. Multiple contractors report in different formats, accountability becomes unclear, and delayed programmes lack a credible recovery path.

What I Deliver

  • Project control frameworks and integrated schedules
  • Progress measurement, cost control, and EVM
  • Risk and change control processes
  • PMO governance structures and reporting standards
  • KPI dashboards and management reporting
  • Review cycles and escalation mechanisms
  • Recovery planning and corrective action
  • Recovery monitoring and re-baselining support

Expected Value

One reliable view of programme performance, clearer accountability, earlier warning of deviations, and a structured path back to control.

How I Help

I establish practical control and governance systems that fit the programme rather than adding bureaucracy, and when recovery is needed I build a diagnosis-led plan with realistic targets.
